# Registration and teams

Everything is configurable per tournament.

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Players per team | Any (2, 3, 4, 6...), optional substitutes |
| Registration mode | Solo, pair, full team, can be combined |
| Team building | Random, balanced by declared level, or manual |
| Constraints | Mixed teams required, pairs kept together, seeds |
| Registration | By the organizer, or through a public link before the tournament |

The organizer can always move a player from one team to another after the draw.
