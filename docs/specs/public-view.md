# Public view, venue display and printouts

A link and a QR code posted in the venue open the tournament on a phone, with no account and no app to install.

## Phone

- "My team": next match, court, time, team to referee.
- Live pool standings and brackets.
- Full schedule by court, filterable by team.
- Updates automatically without reloading, installable on the home screen (PWA).

## Venue display

- Full-screen view for a TV: ongoing matches, scores, upcoming matches, standings.

## Printouts (PDF)

- Poster with a QR code.
- Referee cards or score sheets per match.
